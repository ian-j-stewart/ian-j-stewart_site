Insight is the broader analytical platform I am developing through Fusion Labs. It combines workflow-guided analysis with specialist data and services for export controls, sanctions, research security, strategic technology and related national-security problems.

The platform is broader than the user-facing Insight interface. Its supporting services include structured regulatory and control data, sanctions screening, document and evidence retrieval, knowledge-graph enrichment, classification support, supply-chain and procurement data, and document-intelligence capabilities. These services are designed to work together so that analysis can be grounded in source material, provenance and explicit analytical steps.

The aim is not to replace expert judgment with generic AI. Insight is designed to support expert decision-making by making relevant evidence easier to retrieve, structure and assess, while preserving traceability, uncertainty and human accountability.

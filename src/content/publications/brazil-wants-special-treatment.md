This commentary considers Brazil's proposal for an exception to IAEA safeguards for its nuclear-powered submarine programme, drawing parallels with the AUKUS arrangement for Australia. It discusses the implications for the nonproliferation regime and the broader politics of naval nuclear propulsion.

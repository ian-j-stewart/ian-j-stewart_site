This paper proposes a framework for evaluating the risks that large language models could assist in chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear production processes. It identifies the categories of knowledge and reasoning that are relevant, the evidence needed to assess risk, and the limitations of existing red-teaming and evaluation approaches.

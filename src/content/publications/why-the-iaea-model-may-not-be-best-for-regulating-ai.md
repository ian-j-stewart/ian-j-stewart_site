This commentary examines the limits of borrowing the IAEA's safeguards model for the governance of artificial intelligence. It argues that the technical and institutional characteristics of AI differ significantly from those of nuclear material, and that effective AI governance will require approaches suited to the distributed, fast-moving and dual-use nature of the technology.

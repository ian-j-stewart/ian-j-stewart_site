This chapter considers the future of UN Security Council Resolution 1540 as an instrument for preventing the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. It reflects on the resolution's achievements, the practical obstacles to fuller implementation, and the ways in which the 1540 framework may need to evolve if it is to remain relevant.

This volume brings together contributions assessing the implementation and effectiveness of UN Security Council Resolution 1540 in preventing the proliferation of weapons of mass destruction. It examines reporting, national implementation, international assistance and the political challenges that have shaped the resolution's impact since its adoption in 2004.

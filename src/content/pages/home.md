I am an international-security scholar and practitioner working on the governance of strategically consequential technologies. My work spans nuclear and dual-use technology, export controls, artificial intelligence and compute, semiconductors, research security, sanctions and strategic supply chains. Across these areas, I combine policy and institutional analysis with technical, data-driven and investigative methods.

I am Executive Director of the Washington, DC office of the [James Martin Center for Nonproliferation Studies](https://www.middlebury.edu/institute/people/ian-stewart).

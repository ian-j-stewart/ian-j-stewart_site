I am an international-security scholar, practitioner and founder working on the governance of strategically consequential technologies. My work spans nuclear and dual-use technology, export controls, artificial intelligence and compute, semiconductors, research security, sanctions and strategic supply chains. Across these areas, I combine policy and institutional analysis with technical, data-driven and investigative methods.

I am Executive Director of the Washington, DC office of the [James Martin Center for Nonproliferation Studies](https://www.middlebury.edu/institute/people/ian-stewart) and founder of [Fusion Labs](https://www.fusionlabs.info/), where I develop data, AI and decision-support tools for national security applications. I also maintain [Nonpro Archive](https://www.nonproarchive.com/) as a structured archive of research, case studies and analytical work on nonproliferation, export controls and strategic technology.

title: "Research"
description: "Research on the governance of strategic technology, export controls, AI, compute, semiconductors and nuclear nonproliferation."
heading: "Governing strategic technology"
---

My research examines how states can pursue security and strategic advantage when technological capability is distributed across international supply chains, scientific communities, commercial infrastructure and private organizations. I am particularly interested in the design and implementation of policies intended to control technological diffusion, protect sensitive knowledge and infrastructure, and manage the effects of consequential technologies on international security.
